# Cleverlist Support

Cleverlist is a local-first shopping-list app for organizing everyday shopping. You can keep lists on your device or choose to share a specific list with people you invite through iCloud.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an account?

No. Cleverlist has no app-specific account system. Local lists, catalog entries, locations, preferences, and notification rules are stored on your device.

### How do I share a list?

Open the list's sharing controls, choose iCloud sharing, and invite people with the system share sheet. Only the list you choose to share and its items are shared. Your separate lists, store affiliations, aisle order, and unrelated catalog entries are not shared.

### Do I need iCloud to use Cleverlist?

No. iCloud is optional. You only need it to share a list or receive its changes across invited participants. The normal local-list experience works without it.

### Why is a shared list read-only?

The owner may have given you view-only access, or your invitation's permissions may have changed. Ask the owner to review the share's permissions in their Apple sharing controls.

### How do change notifications work?

If you enable notifications and create a matching rule, Cleverlist can show a local notification when a shared item changes. The notification may display the item's product name. You can change notification permission in system settings and remove rules in Cleverlist.

### How do I remove my data?

Delete individual lists, items, catalog entries, locations, or rules in the app. To remove all local data, delete the app and its data from your device, subject to any backups you control. If you shared a list, stop sharing it or remove participants through the sharing controls; copies already received by participants are under their control.

### The app is not behaving as expected. What can I try?

1. Confirm you are using the latest available Cleverlist and operating-system version.
2. Close and reopen the app.
3. Verify that you are signed in to iCloud if the issue concerns a shared list.
4. Check iCloud sharing permissions with the list owner.
5. If it continues, report the issue as described below.

## Contact support

Open a [new GitHub issue](https://github.com/KibbaFlyer/Schn-ppchenHunters/issues/new/choose) and describe what happened, what you expected, and how to reproduce it. Include the app version and operating-system version when relevant.

GitHub issues are public. Do not attach shopping lists, screenshots containing personal information, CloudKit invitation links, or other sensitive information.
