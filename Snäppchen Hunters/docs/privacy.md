# Cleverlist Data Use & Privacy Information

**Effective date: November 14, 2026**

Cleverlist is designed to keep shopping data local by default. It does not require an app-specific account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or a developer-operated server.

## Information handled by the app

Cleverlist handles information you choose to enter or configure, including shopping lists and list items, custom product and catalog entries, store locations and aisle order, language and theme preferences, and notification rules.

If you choose to share a list through iCloud, the app also handles the shared list's name, items, quantities, completion state, and the product details needed to show those items. Do not add information to a shared list that you would not want the invited participants to see.

If you enable change notifications, Cleverlist may place an item's product name in a notification shown on your device.

## Where information is stored

Your non-shared shopping data is stored in Cleverlist's local app container on your device. Depending on your Apple backup settings, local app data may be included in a device backup managed by Apple.

When you choose iCloud sharing for a list, that list and its items are stored in Apple's CloudKit service in the app's private and shared databases. Access is controlled by the iCloud share and the participants you invite. Apple processes this information under its own privacy terms. Cleverlist does not make shared lists publicly readable.

## Network requests and third parties

The app contacts Apple services when you use iCloud sharing or its change delivery. Apple may receive the usual technical information associated with those requests, such as your IP address, request time, and Apple Account context. The developer does not receive or operate a separate copy of your shopping data.

Cleverlist does not use advertising SDKs, analytics services, or crash-reporting services operated by the developer. It does not sell personal information or use it to track you across apps or websites.

## Your choices and deletion

iCloud sharing and notifications are optional. You can stop sharing a list, remove a participant, or change permissions through the Apple sharing controls. You can change notification permission in your device settings.

Delete local data from within the app, or remove the app and its data to remove the app's local container, subject to backups you control. Stopping a share prevents future access through that share; data already copied or recorded by another participant is under that participant's control.

## Changes and contact

This page will be updated if Cleverlist's data practices change. For privacy questions, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/KibbaFlyer/Schn-ppchenHunters/issues/new/choose) without including personal shopping data or invitation links.
